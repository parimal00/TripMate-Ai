1. JUNGFRAU REGION, SWITZERLAND

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Duration of Stay: 10 Days

Best Time Visited: Early September

Style: Alpine Hiking, Scenic Rail, Nature



[Why It's a Favorite]

The Jungfrau region offers the most dramatically beautiful alpine scenery in Europe. Staying for 10 full days allowed me to explore beyond typical tourist stops and experience silent mountain trails.



[Detailed Activities & Highlights]

- Base Town (Wengen): Stayed in car-free Wengen. It is vastly superior to Interlaken because it sits high on the cliff edge with constant views of the Lauterbrunnen Valley.

- Eiger Trail Hike: Hiked directly beneath the notorious North Face of the Eiger from Eigergletscher down to Alpiglen. The 3-hour hike provides dramatic panoramas of the Grindelwald basin.

- Lauterbrunnen Valley Walk: Strolled the floor of the "Valley of 72 Waterfalls" and explored Trümmelbach Falls—a series of ten glacier-fed waterfalls hidden inside a mountain.

- Schynige Platte Ridge Walk: Took a historic 19th-century cogwheel train up to Schynige Platte for an uncrowded ridge walk overlooking Lakes Thun and Brienz.

2. KYOTO & HAKONE, JAPAN

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Duration of Stay: 14 Days (11 Days Kyoto, 3 Days Hakone)

Best Time Visited: Late October / Early November

Style: Cultural Heritage, Culinary Exploration, Thermal Springs



[Why It's a Favorite]

A two-week stay allowed deep immersion into traditional Japanese architecture, zen gardens, and world-class regional food without rushing.



[Detailed Activities & Highlights]

- Hakone Onsen Stay: Spent 2 nights at Yama-no-Chaya, a secluded traditional ryokan nestled in a ravine. Experiencing a private open-air hot spring (onsen) paired with a multi-course seasonal Kaiseki dinner was an absolute highlight.

- Fushimi Inari at Sunrise: Started the mountain loop at 06:00 AM. Walking through thousands of vermilion Torii gates in complete silence before the crowds arrived was unforgettable.

- Okochi Sanso Villa (Arashiyama): Skipped the crowded main bamboo alley and spent two hours wandering the peaceful private gardens of this historic estate, ending with hot matcha and sweets.

- Evening Walks in Gion & Pontocho: Explored the narrow stone alleyways along the Kamogawa River, sampling local yakitori and seasonal Kyoto dishes.



--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. AMALFI COAST & CAPRI, ITALY

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Duration of Stay: 7 Days

Best Time Visited: Mid-May

Style: Coastal Treks, Gastronomy, Boat Excursions



[Why It's a Favorite]

Perched cliffside villages, fresh seafood, and dramatic Mediterranean coastal views make this one of the most stunning shorelines in the world.



[Detailed Activities & Highlights]

- Sentiero degli Dei (Path of the Gods Hike): A stunning 3.5-hour high-altitude trail from Bomerano down to Nocelle. It offers sweeping views of the entire Amalfi peninsula and Capri.

- Base Town (Praiano): Stayed in Praiano instead of Positano. Praiano is quieter, far less expensive, and offers the single best sunset view across the water toward Capri.

- Private Boat Tour around Capri: Hired a private wooden boat (gozzo) from Marina Grande to swim in hidden sea grottos and escape the commercial ferry crowds.



--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CORE TRAVEL RECOMMENDATIONS & ADVICE

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Avoid Peak Summer: Travel in shoulder seasons (May/September) to avoid brutal heat and dense crowds in Western Europe and East Asia.

- The One Major Activity Rule: Limit yourself to one major planned activity per day. Leave the afternoon free for spontaneous exploration and relaxation.

- Carry-On Only Strategy: Use a single 40L backpack when traveling through Europe or Japan by train. Handling large suitcases on cobblestones or narrow train stairs ruins the experience.
